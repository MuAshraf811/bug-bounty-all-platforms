# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this the most complete bug bounty platform index!

## Adding or editing a platform

1. Fork the repo and create a branch (`add-platform-xyz`).
2. Add the platform to **exactly one** category. Precedence when it could fit several:
   `Government → Web3 → AI → Acquisition → Ecosystem → General`.
3. Fill **every column**: Platform (linked), Region, Programs, Rewards, Notes.
4. Use the Rewards legend: 💰 Cash · ₿ Crypto · 🎁 Swag · 🏅 Points/Rep · 🤝 Kudos-only.
5. Keep entries reasonably ordered within a section.
6. Only add **legitimate, active** platforms with a working official URL. Cite your source in the PR.

## Not accepted

- Platforms facilitating illegal access or selling exploits to non-vendors.
- Dead links, duplicates, or entries with missing data.

## Style

- One platform per row. Keep Notes short (what it's known for).
- Prefer official URLs over blog posts or aggregators.
